Monetary Policy and the Evolution of Modern Central Banking

Welcome to the Central Bank of Armenia’s Onboarding Platform for new staff and board members. This platform introduces you to the evolution of modern monetary policy — from its global intellectual roots to Armenia’s current framework, known as the Prudent Risk Management Approach to Monetary Policy, also referred to as FPAS Mark II.

Designed for new members of the CBA team — whether joining the Board or staff — this platform provides a structured and engaging learning experience. It explains how economic thinking has evolved through decades of global practice, culminating in the CBA’s modern approach to policy under uncertainty.
